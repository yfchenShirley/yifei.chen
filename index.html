ABOUT ME

Learning robots.
Robust behaviour.

Hi, I am Yifei Chen, an Early Research Career Fellow in the Human–Robot Interaction team at CSIRO, Australia.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and robotic manipulation. I study how robots can learn from demonstrations and human feedback to acquire skills that generalize across tasks and changing environments.

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Groningen, where I studied adaptive potential functions for reinforcement learning. My current interests include action chunking for robotic imitation learning, reinforcement learning, resolving causal confusion, and learning from human intervention feedback.

I develop and evaluate learning methods in MuJoCo and CoppeliaSim, collect demonstrations, and deploy policies on physical robots, including ALOHA arms and Baxter.
